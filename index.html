About

The palm has always known how to endure.

Ancient Grove is a multinational company specialized in palm dates. We build enduring brands around a single, humble fruit, turning centuries of farming knowledge into nutritious products delivered to people all over the world.

"The palm tree is like a noble person, for it bears fruit generously and withstands the harshest conditions. And when it dies, it's wood still gives warmth, as a noble person would continue to give even after their death" - Al Jahiz, 9th century scholar and lover of dates.

SUSTAINABILITY

Farming that gives back to the land.

From how we farm down to our packaging, we make sure we're good stewards of this plant earth we all share.
